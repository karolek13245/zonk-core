First-run tips

Treat it well.

A model keeps your phone's chip busy. Three habits keep it happy.

Keep it charged

Long chats draw real power. Plug in before a big session.

Keep it cool, 10–20 °C

A warm phone slows down. Rest it somewhere cool between chats.

Stay on Wi-Fi

Models are big downloads, and hosting needs a steady connection.
